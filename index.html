Hermes

A personal AI assistant, used only by its developer.

Hermes is a private, single-user tool that connects an AI assistant to its developer's own Google Workspace account (Gmail, Calendar, and Drive). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the developer.

Privacy Policy

Hermes is a personal tool used only by its developer to access their own Google account through an AI assistant.

The app accesses Google user data only to perform actions requested by the account owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is processed only on infrastructure controlled by the developer, and OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
